## 🎮 Who We Are

**Studio 1159** is an independent game development studio driven by curiosity, experimentation, and a love for interactive experiences.

We are a small team of creators exploring ideas, building unusual worlds, and turning our imagination into games. We believe that great experiences don't need enormous teams or massive budgets—they need creative freedom, thoughtful design, and the courage to try something different.

From atmospheric experiences to playful experiments, we make games because we love playing them just as much as we love creating them.

## ✦ What We Believe

- **Play is exploration.** Every game is an opportunity to discover something new.
- **Experiment without fear.** Interesting ideas often begin with unconventional approaches.
- **Small teams, big ideas.** Creativity isn't measured by the size of a studio.
- **Make experiences that stay with you.** We want our games to leave players with something to remember, whether it's a feeling, a discovery, or a story.

## 🛠️ How We Create

Our development process is guided by experimentation and collaboration. Depending on the project, we explore:

- **Game Design** — mechanics, player experience, and creative direction.
- **Development** — turning ideas and prototypes into playable experiences.
- **Art & Visuals** — creating worlds with their own identity and atmosphere.
- **Audio & Atmosphere** — using sound and ambience to shape how a game feels.
- **Iteration** — testing, refining, and learning as we build.

We value the process of making games just as much as the finished product.

## 🤝 Collaborate With Us

We believe indie development is better when creative people come together.

We're interested in connecting with artists, programmers, designers, musicians, sound designers, writers, and other creators who want to experiment and make interesting games.

Some collaborations may be unpaid or passion-driven, depending on the project. Any expectations and arrangements should be discussed clearly before work begins.

If you enjoy making games, exploring unusual ideas, and collaborating with a small creative team, we'd love to hear from you.
